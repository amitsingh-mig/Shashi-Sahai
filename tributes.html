REMEMBERED BY RANI PATEL

The person
who was there.

In Rani Patel’s remembrance, Shashi’s presence is a source of quiet reassurance—a friend, a confidant and a companion in Aarohan’s work.

“He gave affection without measuring status, age, gender or social position.”

Rani PatelFounder & President, Aarohan NGO

She remembers someone who listened, who made people feel valued and who offered support without needing recognition. Children trusted him. Friends knew they could rely on him.

That care lives on alongside the photographic archive: in relationships, in shared work, and in the people who carry his memory forward.
