A DIFFERENT KIND OF LEGACYAAROHAN NGO

Showing up.
Staying alongside.

For Shashi, being present mattered. His connection to Aarohan grew from the same instinct that guided his camera: take the time to understand another person’s world.

As a cofounder and treasurer, he worked in service of children, women and vulnerable communities. The recollections preserved on the original memorial site describe patient, practical work built around trust.

01 / CHILDREN & CREATIVITY

A camera can open
a conversation.

Through photography and storytelling, Shashi built relationships with children and their families. Creative work became a way of listening, learning and seeing possibility together.

02 / COMMUNITY & TRUST

comes first.

Aarohan’s reflections recall his willingness to spend time with communities, including in Araku, and to look for practical ways forward with the people involved.
