A PLACE
TO RETURN.

This digital exhibition brings together 31 photographs drawn from Shashi Sahai’s original and companion memorial websites, alongside a portrait of his life and remembrances of his work with people. It is a focused selection to explore at your own pace, rather than a complete record of his career.

01

Where the collection begins

The original memorial provides 26 photographs. Five additional photographs come from The Long Look companion memorial, alongside three documentary images used in the personal chapters. The 31 photographic works are arranged into six collections: People, Everyday life, Heritage, Nature, City & Light and Wildlife. These groupings offer different routes through the same set of images.

Stories bring selected photographs together for a closer look at a theme. Their commentary is an editorial reading of what is visible in the images; it does not claim to recover the photographer’s intentions or the unseen lives of the people pictured.

02

Titles, dates and places

The photograph titles used here are descriptive titles created for this exhibition. They identify a visible subject or action and should not be read as original titles supplied by Shashi Sahai.

Individual dates, locations, assignments and the identities of the people pictured have not been established for this selection. Where those details are unavailable, the exhibition leaves them open. Descriptions stay close to what can be seen rather than assigning a place or a story without evidence.

03

Choose your way through

Start with Collections for six broad themes, Stories for a guided sequence, or The Archive to see all 31 photographs together. The archive can be searched by descriptive title, category or a detail in the image description. Filters narrow the selection, while Grid and Index offer two ways to scan it.

Open a photograph to view it at a larger size. Use the previous and next controls, your keyboard’s arrow keys, or a swipe on a touch screen to continue through the current selection. Escape closes the viewer and returns you to the page.

04

Make the pace your own

Every photograph has a written description. Navigation, gallery controls and the full-screen viewer can be used with a keyboard, and a skip link takes you directly to the main content.

The Motion control in the footer lets you turn off parallax and scroll animation. The exhibition also responds to your device’s reduced-motion preference. The opening sequence can be skipped, and the footer provides a way to replay it.

05

Photographs, words and rights

The original memorial’s copyright credit is retained: © 2026 Media India Group. All rights reserved. Display in this exhibition does not grant a licence to reproduce, distribute or commercially use the photographs or source material.

Biographical information and personal reflections are attributed to their sources. Rani Patel’s remembrance is identified by name; other recollections are presented as accounts from the original memorial, rather than as independently verified records. Links allow you to read those sources in their original context.
