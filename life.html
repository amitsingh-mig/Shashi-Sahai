A LIFE OF ATTENTIONNEW DELHI · THE WORLD

Some stories are made
by the way we live.

For more than 35 years, Shashi Sahai photographed the world with curiosity, patience and a feeling for the people within it.

His work brought him across five continents and into national and international magazines. From heritage and everyday life to portraits and the natural world, his photographs returned again and again to the details that make a moment human.

His photography received a Bronze at Cannes for an advertising project. Yet his contribution extended well beyond professional recognition. As a cofounder and treasurer of Aarohan NGO, he gave his time and attention to children, women and vulnerable communities.

Shashi passed away in New Delhi on 15 August 2026. This collection remembers both the images he made and the care with which he lived.
