Archival Governance & Stewardship

Policies

Standards of archival stewardship, copyright governance, image usage guidelines, and institutional ethics honoring the lifelong photojournalism and humanitarian legacy of Shashi Sahai.

Last Updated: October 2026 • Published by Media India Group

“Preserving three and a half decades of documentary integrity requires transparent, principled stewardship. Our policies ensure Shashi Sahai's photographs and the communities he documented remain honored with dignity, truth, and respect.”

01 Photography and Image Usage

The photographs presented across this memorial archive reflect over 35 years of dedicated field photojournalism, documentary exhibitions, and cultural preservation. We encourage engagement with these works within the following ethical frameworks:

  • Personal, Scholarly & Educational Study: Non-commercial study, personal appreciation, classroom education, and academic research referencing the photographic oeuvre of Shashi Sahai are warmly welcomed.
  • Strict Prohibition of Unauthorized Commercial Use: No photograph, negative scan, exhibition print, or digital derivative may be reproduced, sold, licensed, repackaged, or utilized for commercial marketing, advertising, physical merchandise, or paid publishing without explicit prior written authorization from Media India Group and the estate representatives.
  • Integrity of the Works: Cropping, digital manipulation, recoloring, automated AI training usage, or misleading contextual reframing that alters the documentary veracity or intent of the original photographs is strictly prohibited.

02 Copyright and Intellectual Property

All original photographic works, film negatives, archival prints, curated essays, documentary narratives, visual arrangements, and website interface assets are protected under the Indian Copyright Act, 1957, the Berne Convention, and international intellectual property laws:

  • Photographic Estate: Copyright in all photographs captured by Shashi Sahai remains vested in his estate and Media India Group, unless explicitly denoted under joint campaign archives (such as the 2012 Cannes Lions Bronze collaborative showcase).
  • Editorial Texts & Website Design: All accompanying biographical tributes, curatorial notes, website layouts, custom graphics, and code architecture are the proprietary intellectual property of Media India Group (© 2026).
  • Trademarks & Brand Marks: The Shashi Sahai signature monogram, brand calligraphy, and Media India Group emblems may not be used without prior written consent.

03 Content Attribution and Credits

Authentic acknowledgment preserves historical record and honors the communities captured in each frame. When quoting textual excerpts or referencing specific works in journalistic or educational articles, proper attribution must be maintained:

  • Standard Photo Credit Line: “Photograph by Shashi Sahai / Courtesy of Media India Group & Shashi Sahai Memorial Archive.”
  • Community & Partner Acknowledgments: We extend profound gratitude to Rani Patel, the field coordinators and youth at Aarohan NGO, and the community members across Delhi, Banaras, and Araku whose shared trust and lived stories form the foundation of these collections.
  • Historical Accuracy: Citations should include the title, year of capture (where documented), and exhibition series to safeguard archival context.

04 Privacy and Data Protection

This platform is dedicated to public memory, education, and cultural appreciation. In accordance with our privacy-first philosophy:

  • Data Minimization: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or track personal visitor data. The website operates without commercial tracking pixels, third-party advertising cookies, or behavioral surveillance.
  • Interactive Tributes: Contributions shared to the public Tribute Wall are moderated to ensure respect and empathy. Personal submissions are stored securely and treated in accordance with our standalone Privacy Policy.
  • Technical Logs: Transient server diagnostics are retained purely for uptime stability, threat mitigation, and secure performance monitoring.

05 External Links

Throughout this platform, hyperlinks are provided to official external destinations connected to Shashi Sahai's philanthropic work, including:

  • Aarohan NGO: aarohanngo.org/shashi-sahai/ — highlighting his co-founding role and enduring social impact.

While we carefully curate external references, Media India Group does not control or assume legal liability for third-party websites, their evolving content, or their independent privacy protocols. Visitors accessing external links do so subject to those domains' respective terms.
