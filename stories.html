SIX WAYS TO LOOK A LITTLE LONGER

INSIDE
THE FRAME.

A photograph holds more than its first impression. Follow a gesture, a line, a change in light. These six visual essays take a slower route through the collection.

A NOTE ON THESE STORIES

These essays are new readings of the photographs in this archive. They describe what can be seen and offer ways to look; they are not Shashi’s own words or accounts of how the photographs were made.
