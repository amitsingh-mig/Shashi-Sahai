Website Governance & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Our commitment to respecting visitor privacy, photographic copyright, and transparent data practices on this memorial and archival website.

Last Updated: October 2026 • Published by Media India Group

“This platform is dedicated to preserving the life's work of Shashi Sahai. We hold visitor trust and photographic stewardship with the highest degree of respect and integrity.”

1. Purpose of this Memorial Platform

This website serves as an archival tribute and educational portfolio commemorating the life, photojournalism, and philanthropic contributions of Shashi Sahai (1961–2026). Maintained by Media India Group, the site provides public access to archival photographs, timeline retrospectives, and an interactive Tribute Wall for colleagues, friends, and well-wishers.

2. Information We Collect & Process

We adhere to a strict data minimization policy. Because this is a static informational memorial website, we do not require user account registration, login credentials, or commercial payment transactions.

  • Zero Tracking Cookies & Ad Trackers: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site behavioral tracking scripts, or commercial retargeting beacons.
  • Tribute Submissions: When you voluntarily submit a message to the Tribute Wall, you may provide your name, an optional relationship or professional role, and your message. By default, submitted tribute entries are rendered on the page and saved locally in your own browser's storage (localStorage) to personalize your visit.
  • Server Log Data: Like standard web servers, anonymous technical data (such as browser type, operating system, and IP address) may be momentarily recorded in standard hosting access logs solely for diagnostic stability and security monitoring.

3. Photographic Copyright & Archival Rights

All photographs, negatives, documentary scans, and multimedia assets presented on this website are the copyrighted intellectual property of the Estate of Shashi Sahai and Media India Group, unless specifically credited otherwise.

  • These works are presented for archival viewing, journalistic appreciation, and personal commemoration.
  • Unauthorized commercial reproduction, redistribution, modification, syndication, or automated web scraping/harvesting of these photographic assets without prior written authorization is strictly prohibited.

4. Third-Party External Hyperlinks

This website contains links to external online resources, including the official memorial Instagram profile (@shashiphotographs) and the official website of Aarohan NGO (aarohanngo.org), the grassroots organization Shashi Sahai cofounded and served as treasurer.

Please note that once you navigate away from this website to external third-party domains, their respective privacy policies and terms apply. We encourage you to review the privacy notices of any external site you visit.

5. Children's Privacy

This website is designed for general audiences and contains no offensive or commercial marketing material. We do not knowingly collect identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect technical enhancements or archival updates. Any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated “Last Updated” timestamp.
