35 Years of Photojournalism & Philanthropy

Shashi Sahai — veteran photojournalist, photographer and philanthropist — passed away in New Delhi on August 15, 2026, after a prolonged battle with cancer. He was 66. For over 35 years, his lens traversed heritage streets, quiet misty landscapes, timeless cultural celebrations and intimate human moments across five continents.

A prolific contributor to leading national and international magazines, he received the Bronze at Cannes for an advertising project. Yet more than his accolades, colleagues and friends remember a man who could find a story in the seemingly ordinary — who stood behind the lens not for recognition, but because it was where he felt most at home.

As cofounder of Aarohan NGO, he worked quietly in service of children, women and vulnerable communities. He was the person who called. The person who showed up. The person who remembered.
