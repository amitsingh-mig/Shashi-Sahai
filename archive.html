Our Journey

Three decades, told frame by frame

Every era leaves its own light. Here is how Shashi Sahai's lens has moved with the world, one decade at a time.

1991

The First Frame

A single darkroom and a hand-loaded roll of film. Shashi Sahai Studio begins as a modest documentary studio.

2001

Beyond the Studio

Travel and culture assignments take the lens across five continents, building the first archive of portraits.

2012

Digital Horizon

The archive crosses into the digital age without losing its documentary instinct — grain gives way to detail.

2026

Three Decades On

Over a million frames later, Shashi Sahai stands as a living archive of people, places and the stories between them.
